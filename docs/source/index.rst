=====================
Welcome
=====================

Welcome to the documentation for SATHI: Self Reported Assessment and Tracking for Health Insights. SATHI is an open source application developed for the express purpose of allowing healthcare workers to monitor patient reported outcomes and patient experience measures. SATHI does not dictate specific type of measure for this but is designed to accommodate most patient reported outcome measures (PROMs) or patient reported experience measures (PREMs). 

The documentation has been divided into three sections:  

#. **Patient documentation**: This is focused on the patients who will be using the system to respond to the PROMs/PREMs in the system. 
#. **Healthcare worker documentation**: This is focused on healthcare workers who will be using it for evaluating and assessing responses provided by the patients.
#. **Developer documentation**: This is focused on developers who are interested in installing and extending this system.
